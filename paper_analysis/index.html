The Core Causal Chain

Read the thesis left to right. The papers are not stock tips by themselves. They are evidence that specific physical bottlenecks are becoming economically visible.

1. Bigger models

Training and serving move more data across GPUs, memory, storage, and power systems.

2. Bottleneck appears

Communication stalls, HBM capacity, checkpoint I/O, or power swings become the limiting reagent.

3. Buyer pain

Hyperscalers either buy more hardware or buy better infrastructure to use existing hardware harder.

4. Company exposure

The winners sell the scarce layer: networking, power conditioning, HBM, storage, or accelerators.

5. Trade filter

Only act when thesis, chart, valuation, and event risk are not fighting each other.

The Numbers That Make This Concrete

These are the load-bearing findings from the deep read. They turn the report from "AI infrastructure is tight" into specific investable bottlenecks.

Interconnect wall78%

MoE all-to-all communication reached 78% of step time at 8 nodes. That is why AVGO/MRVL/NVDA matter.

Collective compression9.65x

NCCLZ speedup over vanilla NCCL on inter-node collectives. If it works in production, bandwidth demand is effectively compressed.

Power resonance0.2-3 Hz

Training load swings land in utility-sensitive frequency ranges. This is the reason ETN is the highest-quality early thesis.

Storage paradox92.2%

NFS RPC slot wait was 92.2% of checkpoint save latency. The storage bottleneck is protocol/software, not raw RoCE speed.

HBM extension99.6%

ZipServ found contiguous BF16 exponent windows in 99.6% of matrices, enabling fixed-length lossless compression.

KV cache14x

MoE-nD showed 14x KV-cache compression at matched accuracy, cutting HBM pressure for long-context inference.

Mixed GPU training97%

HetCCL reached up to 97% mixed-vendor efficiency. This is the concrete AMD adoption unlock.

Agent serving967%

AMPD improved SLO attainment over pure disaggregated serving by up to 967%, validating hybrid inference routing.

Executive Read

The strongest paper-to-market signal is not necessarily the best immediate trade.

Best thesis plus chart: AVGO, MRVL, MU, NVDA. These have direct exposure to interconnect or memory pressure and currently show positive technical structure.

Best contrarian thesis but weak chart: ETN. The power-stabilization paper is a real early signal, but ETN is below key trend measures. I would wait for a reclaim near 419-425 before treating it as an add.

Most important correction: VST is Vistra Energy, not VAST Data. VAST Data is private. So the checkpoint-storage thesis cannot be bought through VST; the public proxies are PSTG, NTAP, and to a lesser extent SMCI.
